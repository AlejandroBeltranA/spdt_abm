02 · Climate inputs

Climate inputs

Outdoor temperature at hourly resolution is the exogenous driver of heating load. Daily means would smooth out the morning and evening peaks that determine peak demand and bill impact.

What's in it

variable 2 metre air temperature at hourly resolution. Temperature alone explains the bulk of heating variance once SAP and occupancy are known.

spatial 20 grid points on a 0.1° lattice across the city. Each dwelling is matched to its nearest point by haversine distance, so coastal and inner-city dwellings see different forcings.

coverage 2020 to 2039, roughly 3.5M hourly rows per scenario. The horizon is set by the policy outputs we care about (2030 net-zero milestones, 2035 boiler ban, 2040 grid decarbonisation).

historical ERA5 reanalysis from ECMWF Copernicus CDS for the 2020 to 2022 backcast used in calibration and validation.

forward Climate-DT digital-twin projections from Destination Earth, three SSP3-7.0 realisations (ICON, IFS-FESOM, IFS-NEMO). The three-model envelope is what gives us climate uncertainty bands on the demand forecast.

134,503 dwellings, each coloured by the 2m air temperature of its matched ERA5 grid point on 12 December 2022. The colour gradient shows which dwellings sit in the city's coldest pockets. Those are the same households the policy module flags first.

07 · Calibration

Calibration

Parameters without a direct empirical anchor (baseline draw, person-load constant, area exponent, schedule modifiers) are fit against SERL intraday profiles by sequential Monte-Carlo, with the DESNZ annual total as a soft constraint.

What gets calibrated

baseline_anchor hourly non-climate appliance draw

person_kwh_h per-person occupancy load

area_exponent sublinear floor-area scaling

asleep_factor overnight occupancy reduction

wealth_modifiers ±25 to 30% by income band

heating_k SAP-to-sensitivity slope

How the fit works

SMC samples 600 candidate parameter sets from plausibility-bounded priors, scores each on SERL intraday RMSE × DESNZ annual MAPE, keeps the top decile, and resamples around them. Three rounds.

Fit holds out 2022 as the test year. Parameters are frozen before scenario analysis.

Calibrated monthly ratio, ABM over SERL

Green = within ±10%, orange = within ±25%, red = outside. Gas-heated homes sit in-band for most of the year; the April / October shoulder months remain the hardest to fit and frame the next round of work.

09 · Policy scenarios

Policy scenarios

Both scenarios deploy heat pumps. The difference is who gets one. Targeting logic shapes programme performance as much as the intervention itself.

hp_social_rent

Targets social-rent tenure. 50% uptake in cohort. Easier delivery through portfolio landlords; addresses fuel poverty directly.

Eligible: 23,540 · Adopters: 11,770

hp_top_users

Targets the top 10% consumers. 20% uptake, regardless of tenure. Higher per-dwelling impact; harder to coordinate.

Eligible: 9,771 · Adopters: 1,954
